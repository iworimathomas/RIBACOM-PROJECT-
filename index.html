About RIBACOM

RIVERS BAYELSA COMMUNITY IN DIASPORA – THE GAMBIA


RIBACOM is a community organization bringing together people of Rivers and Bayelsa origin residing in The Gambia.


The community promotes peace, unity, progress, welfare, cultural identity and mutual support among its members.


Our Motto

PEACE • UNITY • PROGRESS
