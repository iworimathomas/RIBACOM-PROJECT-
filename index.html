About RIBACOM

RIVERS BAYELSA COMMUNITY IN DIASPORA – THE GAMBIA (RIBACOM) is a community organisation bringing together people of Rivers and Bayelsa heritage living in The Gambia.


RIBACOM promotes peaceful coexistence, unity, cultural identity, welfare and community development.


The organisation provides a platform through which members can participate in community activities, support one another and contribute positively to society.

RIBACOM Constitution

The approved RIBACOM Constitution governs the organisation, membership, leadership, welfare and community administration.

Organisational Structure

RIBACOM is governed through its approved constitutional structures and democratic community processes.


Executive Committee

  1. Chairman
  2. Vice-Chairman
  3. Secretary General
  4. Assistant Secretary General
  5. Treasurer
  6. Public Relations Officer
  7. Welfare Officer / Provost

Oversight Committee

The oversight structure provides advisory support in the areas of Legal, Protocol, Youth and Cultural matters.


Council of Elders

The Council of Elders provides experience, guidance and community counsel in accordance with the constitution.


General Meetings

The regular general meeting is held on the last Sunday of the month at 4:00 PM, subject to the approved constitutional arrangements.
