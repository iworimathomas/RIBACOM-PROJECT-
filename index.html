Rivers Bayelsa Community in Diaspora – The Gambia

Who We Are

Rivers Bayelsa Community in Diaspora – The Gambia (RIBACOM) is a community organisation representing members of Rivers and Bayelsa origin residing in The Gambia.


The community provides a platform for unity, welfare, cultural preservation, mutual support and community development.
