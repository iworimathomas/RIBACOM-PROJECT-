About RIBACOM

RIVERS BAYELSA COMMUNITY IN DIASPORA – THE GAMBIA

RIBACOM is a voluntary community association established to promote unity, welfare, cultural identity, mutual support and constructive engagement among Rivers and Bayelsa people connected with The Gambia.
