About RIBACOM

Rivers Bayelsa Community in Diaspora – The Gambia.

Who We Are

Rivers Bayelsa Community in Diaspora – The Gambia (RIBACOM) is a community platform bringing together members of Rivers and Bayelsa origin in The Gambia.

Our Purpose

To promote unity, mutual support, community development, cultural identity and peaceful coexistence among our members.

Our Mission

To build a united and supportive Rivers and Bayelsa community through service, cooperation and responsible community participation.

Our Values

Truth • Unity • Service
